Understanding South Asian Wedding Gold Weights

Gold jewellery plays a treasured role in Bengali, Pakistani, and Indian weddings — from bridal necklaces and bangles to mahr gifts. Across the UK and South Asia, jewellers quote weights in grams, troy ounces, tolas, bori, masha, and ratti:

  • 1 Troy Ounce (oz t) = 31.1035 grams
  • 1 Tola = 11.6638 grams
  • 1 Masha = 1/12 Tola (0.9720 grams)
  • 1 Ratti = 1/96 Tola (0.1215 grams)

FAQs

Gold Price & Zakat FAQs Questions

How is the live gold price per gram, tola, and bori calculated?

Our calculator pulls the live global XAU spot price in USD per troy ounce and converts it into GBP, USD, EUR, AED, BDT, INR, or PKR using daily foreign exchange rates, then scales by your selected purity (14k to 24k).

What purity is typical for Bengali, Pakistani, and Indian bridal gold jewellery?

Most traditional South Asian wedding jewellery sets in the UK are crafted in 22k gold (91.7% pure) or 21k gold (87.5% pure), while investment bars and coins are typically 24k (99.9%+ pure).

How does the Zakat Nisab threshold work for wedding gold?

The classical gold Nisab threshold is 87.48 grams (7.5 tolas) of pure 24k gold under the Hanafi school, or 85 grams under the majority scholarly view. Toggle "Show Zakat Implications" inside either calculator to see pure-gold equivalent weight and an estimated 2.5% Zakat guide.

Does this gold price include jewellery making charges or UK retail markups?

No — the figures shown reflect raw bullion spot market value. High-street Asian jewellers in London (such as Green Street, Southall, or Hatton Garden) typically add craftsmanship/making charges and VAT where applicable.
