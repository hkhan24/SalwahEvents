Bespoke Backdrops Designed Around Your Venue

The wedding stage is the visual centrepiece of your entire celebration — framing every family portrait, couple entrance, and cherished speech. Rather than offering tired cookie-cutter templates, our designers measure your chosen venue and craft a bespoke wedding stage decoration matched to your outfits, florals, and room proportions.

Whether you prefer crisp ivory roses with warm brushed gold arches, rich velvet drapery with crystal chandeliers, or vibrant marigolds and brass lanterns for a Mehndi or Gaye Holud evening, every piece is delivered, rigged safely, and inspected well before your first guest arrives.

Lighting, Walkways & Cohesive Room Styling

Great decor relies on great lighting. We pair architectural LED uplighting, warm spotlights on the couple, and subtle fairy-light canopies so your photographer captures rich, flattering tones throughout dinner and rukhsati.

  • Couple Seating & Sweetheart Tables: Luxury tufted sofas, carved gold chairs, and candlelit top-table floral runners.
  • Entrance & Walkway Framing: Mirrored or ivory carpeted aisles, elevated floral plinths, and personalised welcome signage.
  • One-Team Catering + Decor Advantage: Pair stage styling with our 100% HMC Halal Asian wedding catering or all-inclusive Platinum Package for one point of contact and streamlined venue access.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you create bespoke wedding stage designs in London, Essex, and Kent?

Yes. Every wedding stage decoration we build is tailored to your colour palette, venue dimensions, and cultural ceremony — whether you envision a lush ivory floral wall, modern gold geometric arch, colourful Mehndi stage, serene Nikah backdrop, or luxury Walima setup.

What is included in your wedding stage decoration service?

Our stage packages include the custom backdrop structure, fresh or luxury silk floral installations, couple seating (sweetheart sofa or throne chairs), stage flooring/carpeting, dramatic uplighting and spotlights, plus optional matching walkway pillars, entrance arches, and guest table centrepieces.

Can we bundle wedding stage decoration with Halal catering?

Yes! Bundling catering and stage decoration with Salwah Events saves venue coordination stress and unlocks better combined pricing. Our Platinum Package (from £17,500 for 200 guests) includes full Gold-tier HMC Halal catering, bespoke luxury stage decor, table centrepieces, lighting, and dedicated event management.

Do you style traditional Nikah, Mehndi, Gaye Holud, and Mandap ceremonies?

Absolutely. We design culturally respectful Nikah partitions and floral backdrops, vibrant marigold and jewel-toned Mehndi / Gaye Holud stages with jhoola swings, grand Walima reception stages, and bespoke Mandaps.

Which London venues do you set up wedding stages at?

Our decor crew installs regularly across East, North, West, and South London, Essex, and Kent — including Meridian Grand, The City Pavilion, The Willows, Mayfair Venue, The Royal Regency, Ariana Banqueting Hall, Palm Tree, Oasis, and private marquees.
