FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Asian wedding catering cost per person in London?

Our packages start from £25 per person for the Silver Package (3 starters, 4 mains, pilau rice, plain rice or naan, 1 dessert, crockery, cutlery, and waiting staff) and £40 per person for our most popular Gold Package (adding welcome drinks, canapés, 4 starters including fish, 4 premium mains, biryani or rice, 2 desserts, glassware, and a floor supervisor). Our all-inclusive Platinum Package starts from £17,500 for 200 guests.

Is all of your food 100% HMC Certified Halal?

Yes. We are a dedicated Halal caterer and source all meat and poultry exclusively from HMC-certified suppliers. Our kitchens operate under strict Halal procedures so you and your guests can dine with complete peace of mind.

How many guests can Salwah Events cater for?

We cater smoothly for intimate Nikah lunches, Aqiqahs, and Mehndis starting from 50 guests right up to grand banqueting hall weddings and Walimas for 1,000+ guests across Greater London, Essex, and Kent.

Which South Asian cuisines do you offer?

Our chefs specialise in authentic Bengali (Bangladeshi), Pakistani, and Indian cuisine — from Lamb Kacchi Biryani, Beef Kala Bhuna, and Chicken Roast to Lamb Bhuna, Chicken Karahi, Chapli Kebabs, Papri Chaat, and warm Gulab Jamun. Mixed-heritage menus can also be tailored to suit both families.

Can we arrange a menu consultation and food sampling before booking?

Yes. Get in touch by phone, WhatsApp, or our quote form to arrange a complimentary consultation and food sampling so you and your family can agree on spice levels, dishes, and presentation before confirming your date.

Do you cater at dry-hire venues, hotels, and marquees across London, Essex, and Kent?

Yes. Alongside preferred London banqueting halls such as The City Pavilion, Meridian Grand, The Willows, and Mayfair Venue, we bring full catering equipment, crockery, glassware, and uniformed staff to dry-hire estates, hotels, and marquees across London, Essex, and Kent.
